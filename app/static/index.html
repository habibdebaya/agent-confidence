Method

Reviewer groups and an uncertainty reserve

Repeated submissions and relationships between reviewer addresses can create dependence among ratings. The method assigns one contribution to each observed group and adjusts the aggregate for an explicit reserve of hypothetical adverse sources.

Mathematical derivation

Rating eligibilityThe calculation uses active starred ratings with values in [0, 100]. Other feedback types are excluded.

Group contributionsEach group contributes its minimum admitted rating. Groups linked to the agent's current owner are excluded.

Regularization for limited evidenceTotal support is divided by the number of groups plus four. The report derives this reserve construction and examines its sensitivity.

Interpretation and limitations

The score is an uncalibrated index of support from recorded quality ratings. Prior payment matches are reported as contextual evidence and have no numerical weight. The guarantees are conditional on observed group assignments; coordinated reviewers with undetected relationships can inflate the score.
