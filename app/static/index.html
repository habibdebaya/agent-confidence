      What the colors mean

        Wallets are linked by funding when one wallet gave several reviewers their first funds, when one reviewer funded another, or when the same exchange or bridge funded them within a minute and they reviewed the same agent. A link shows a relationship on chain. It does not show wrongdoing. Report a wrong label
